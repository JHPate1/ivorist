ABOUT IVORIST

Hardware and software belong together.

Ivorist is a small product development studio working across electronics, embedded systems, and software. We started it because we wanted to work on the whole product, not just one part of it.

WHY WE STARTED

Products break at the seam.

Hardware and software often come from separate teams with separate timelines. The problems show up where the two meet: a board designed before the software requirements settle, an app built without knowing what the device can actually do. Both mistakes surface late, when a fix means a respin or a rebuild.

Ivorist keeps both sides in one studio and one conversation. The person routing the board knows what the firmware needs. The person writing the firmware knows what the board can afford, down to the milliamp.

That's the work: products designed as one thing, from the first schematic to the release build.

HOW WE APPROACH PROJECTS

What we hold ourselves to.

01

Understand the full product

Before schematic capture or a line of code, we write down what the device does, what powers it, and what it talks to. Requirements first; parts second.

02

Keep communication direct

You talk to the people doing the work. Decisions about pinouts, protocols, and timelines happen with the engineer holding the schematic, not through a chain of forwarded messages.

03

Test what we build

Boards come back from fab and go straight on the bench. Firmware runs on the hardware it will ship in. Every board gets bench time before it gets boxed.
