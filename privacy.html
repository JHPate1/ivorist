PRIVACY POLICY

What we collect, and what we don't.

Last updated November 2026. This page explains what ivorists.com collects, how that information is used, and how to have it deleted.

01. What we collect

We collect what you send us. If you email team@ivorists.com or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, and whatever you tell us about your company and your project. That usually includes a product description, a timeline, and sometimes a budget range. Please don't send trade secrets in a first message.

If you join a launch list on a project page, the form sends exactly two things: your email address and the project you selected. Nothing else is stored.

Our host keeps standard server logs: IP address, browser type, requested pages, and timestamps. Those logs exist for security and abuse prevention. We do not run advertising networks and we do not use third-party ad trackers.

02. How we use it

Contact information is used to reply to you, evaluate whether a project is a good fit, and keep the conversation moving if it turns into client work. Launch-list emails are used for one thing: telling you when that project launches.

Aggregate log data helps us keep the site secure and understand which pages people actually read. We do not sell your information, rent it out, or use it to build advertising profiles.

03. Project confidentiality

Rough ideas arrive by email often, and we treat them with care. If you describe an unreleased product to us, we keep that description confidential: we don't share it, publish it, or carry it into anyone else's project. That is a matter of professional respect, and it has held since the day we started.

This early courtesy is not a substitute for a contract. Before any real engineering work begins, we sign a mutual NDA and record the engagement in a Master Services Agreement and a Statement of Work, which set out the formal confidentiality, intellectual property, and delivery terms.

04. Cookies and third-party services

ivorists.com is a static website. We don't set advertising or analytics cookies, and the pages work fine with cookies blocked.

Hosting, DNS, and the contact and launch-list endpoints run on Cloudflare. Form submissions are processed by our own code and emailed to team@ivorists.com; launch-list emails are stored in a private database we control. If we ever add analytics, it will be an aggregate, privacy-respecting tool, and we will describe it on this page first.

05. Data retention and your rights

Inquiry emails are kept as long as the conversation is active, and for a reasonable period after, in case you get back in touch. Launch-list emails are kept until the project launches and shortly after, when the list is cleared. Server logs rotate on our host's standard schedule.

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it. Email team@ivorists.com from the address you used, and we will confirm and act on the request, except for records we are required to keep for contracts, invoices, or law.

Questions about this policy go to team@ivorists.com.
