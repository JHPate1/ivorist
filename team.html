THE TEAM

The people behind Ivorist.

Four founders, four territories: product and design, hardware, software, operations. Small enough that everybody touches the product. Nothing gets lost between departments, because there are no departments.

R

Robin

CO-FOUNDER / PRODUCT & DESIGN

Turns product ideas into screens: user flows, wireframes, interfaces, and the decisions about what ships now and what waits. Asks the awkward questions early, while the answers are still cheap. If a screen ever confuses you, say so. That's the feedback Robin actually wants.

J

Jyot

CO-FOUNDER / HARDWARE LEAD

Captures the schematic, routes the board, and runs the first power-on at the bench. Owns component selection, power budgets, and the production BOM. Wants every board to work on the first spin, and usually gets it.

MD

Mohammed Daniyal

CO-FOUNDER / SOFTWARE LEAD

Writes the firmware on the device and the software behind it: backends, APIs, and the contracts that keep both sides honest. Tests on real boards, not emulators. Signs off when a build survives the bench, the field, and a week of nobody touching it.

U

Umar

CO-FOUNDER / OPERATIONS

Orders the parts, chases the deliveries, and keeps timelines honest and clients informed. Want to know where your project really stands? Ask him.
