The plan below ↓

Three promises, pinned to the wall.

Traditional tools break a thought into dialogs. Strova keeps it whole, and plays it back roughly 20x smoother on the same hardware.

01

Flow

Animation by gesture, for strokes and objects alike. The timing of your hand is the law of the scene; editing tools refine that law, never replace it.

02

Custom brushes

Brushes are yours to make: shape, texture, feel, behavior. Tune one for your project, keep it portable, share it if you like. A community's brush kit is an inheritance, not a subscription.

03

Rapid exporting

Getting work out is never slower than making it. Exports run against the same render the preview uses, so the file you share looks exactly like the motion you made.
