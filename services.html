PROJECT WORKFLOW

From an idea to a working build.

01

Understand

Scope the product: what it does, who holds it, what it costs to make. Lock the requirements, the power budget, and the certification targets before anything gets drawn.

02

Plan

Architecture across both worlds: block diagrams, pin assignments, API contracts, and a first BOM estimate. The plan is written down before the PCB gets routed.

03

Develop

Schematic and layout. Firmware on dev kits. Mock APIs while the fab house runs, and app screens against real data. Every board gets bench-tested the day it lands.

04

Review

Compare the build against the requirements from step 01. What works ships, what doesn't gets a ticket, and the next revision starts with a shorter list.
