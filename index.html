Who we are

Why we started Ivorist

We kept running into the same problem from both sides: hardware companies that can't write a good line of software, and software companies that won't go near a schematic. One side waits on the other, timelines slip, and the product pays for it.

So we learned each other's side of the wall, properly. The person laying out your board is on the call when your app's first version gets sketched, and nobody here treats either half like it's somebody else's job.

01

One roof, both halves

The board, its firmware, the app, the back end. One small team owns all of it, start to finish.

02

No translators

You talk to whoever writes your code or routes your traces. Answers come back quickly because the person you're asking is the person doing the work.

03

Boring beats clever

We read each other's code, re-check our own, and argue over a component choice before committing to it. Not glamorous. It's just why the product keeps working.

How we work

How a project actually runs.

  1. 01

    Discover

    It starts with questions, some of them slightly annoying. Who is this actually for? What would "done" look like? We don't pick up tools until the answers are agreed.

  2. 02

    Design

    Schematics, system architecture and screens are designed together. Obvious, right? Most teams skip it, which is how an app discovers the board's quirks halfway to launch.

  3. 03

    Build

    Real boards, real code, short loops. Prototype, test, break it on purpose, fix it, go again. You see working progress at every step instead of one big reveal.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    We hand it over documented and tested, then stay reachable. Launch day is when a product usually needs the most help, not the least.
