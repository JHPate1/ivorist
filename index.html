WHAT WE DO

Two sides of the same product;

A device is more than its circuit board. The firmware, software, and systems around it matter just as much.

Ivorist builds across the full product: schematic capture and PCB layout, firmware on the microcontroller, then the apps, backends, and API contracts that connect it all.

Decisions that touch both sides get made early. A pinout settled in week one saves a board respin in week six.

PROJECT 001 IN DEVELOPMENT

Archivist

A local-first document research tool that helps people find information in their own files and draw conclusions grounded in them.

The current build focuses on document processing and semantic retrieval: extracting text, indexing it locally, and finding passages related to your question. Retrieved passages feed a locally run model, so answers come from your documents and stay traceable to their sources.
